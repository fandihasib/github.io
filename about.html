Biography

Broadcaster by training. Educator by practice.

Fandi Hasib is an Indonesian news anchor, producer, public speaker and language educator. His broadcast career has included TVRI, SINDO TV, TRANS7 and iNews, where he worked across presenting, reporting, interviewing, production and voice-over.

Beyond the newsroom, he has taught Indonesian to international learners through Indonesia's Ministry of Education and Culture. His overseas assignments have included institutions and diplomatic missions in the Philippines, Russia and Uzbekistan.

He also develops speakers and language ambassadors through training in message structure, critical thinking, vocal delivery and public presence.
